───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  RESEARCH QUESTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Do existing multi-agent OPE methods preserve their ability to rank
  coordination policies when the team composition or communication
  structure changes between data collection and evaluation?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  SUB-QUESTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Can uncertainty-aware selective evaluation detect when policy
  selection is no longer statistically defensible?
